- (decision by user) Decision 3b: playoff Match rows ARE persisted (schedule/venue/result). Participant slots of a downstream round (final, 3rd-place) are DERIVED from upstream results UNTIL that match itself has a result, then its pairing is frozen. Editing an upstream result before the downstream match is played re-derives the pairing (FR-20); after, it does not.
